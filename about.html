About ZenMatch Games

Bringing centuries of Chinese puzzle tradition to the modern web

Our Mission

ZenMatch Games was born from a simple belief: the ancient wisdom and entertainment of Chinese puzzle culture deserves to be shared with the world. Our mission is to preserve, digitize, and freely share these timeless games with a global audience.

From the strategic tile-matching of Mahjong to the logic of Klotski (Hua Rong Dao), each game on our platform represents a piece of cultural heritage that has entertained and challenged minds for centuries — in some cases, over two thousand years.

⚖️ Values

What We Stand For

🌎

Free for Everyone

No paywalls, no premium tiers, no ads. Every game, every level — completely free, forever. We believe cultural heritage should not be locked behind a paywall.

🎨

Cultural Authenticity

We respect the origins of every game. Hanzi Match teaches real Chinese character structure. Klotski preserves the Three Kingdoms narrative. Mahjong Solitaire stays true to traditional tile design.

⚡

Instant & Seamless

Every game loads in a flash and runs smoothly on any device. Just open and play — no waiting, no hassle. Immerse yourself in the world of ancient Chinese puzzles.
