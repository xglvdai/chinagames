How to Play Mahjong Solitaire

The complete guide to mastering the world's most popular Chinese tile-matching puzzle

🚀 Objective

Clear the entire board by matching pairs of identical tiles. The game is won when no tiles remain. Each level increases in complexity — can you beat all 10?

🎲 How to Play

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Find free tiles: A tile is "free" when it has no tile directly touching it on both the left AND right sides. Tiles on the outer edges of a stack are free on at least one side.
  2. Select a tile: Click on a free tile to highlight it — it will glow with a golden border.
  3. Find its match: Click on another free tile with the exact same symbol. Both tiles must be identical.
  4. Clear the pair: If the match is valid, both tiles disappear and you earn points. The pair count updates immediately.
  5. Continue matching: Keep finding and matching pairs until the board is completely empty.

What Makes a Tile "Free"?

A tile is free to select only if both its left edge and right edge are open. A tile with another tile sitting on top of it is also blocked. The visual highlight system helps you identify eligible tiles — valid tiles will have a subtle glow when you hover over them.

🏆 Scoring System

  • Base match: +10 points per pair
  • Combo bonus: +5 × combo count bonus for consecutive matches within 3 seconds
  • Time bonus: Faster completion earns higher leaderboard ranking — time is the primary tiebreaker
  • Level completion: Bonus points awarded for clearing a level under the par time
⏰ Combo Tip: Chain matches rapidly to build massive combos. A 5-match combo nets you an extra 75 points on top of the base 50!

🛠️ Special Buttons

  • 💡 Hint: Highlights a valid matching pair on the board. Essential when you feel stuck. Use sparingly — each hint slightly reduces your final time bonus.
  • 🔀 Shuffle: Randomly rearranges all remaining tiles into a new layout. Critical when no valid moves exist. Can be used up to 3 times per level.
  • ↩️ Undo: Reverts your last matched pair. No point penalty — use it to experiment with different matching orders.
  • ⏸ Pause: Freezes the timer. Great for taking breaks without affecting your score.
  • 🔄 Restart: Resets the current level completely. Your best time for the level is preserved.

🂠 Tile Symbols Guide

Mahjong uses 34 unique tile types across several suits. Knowing these helps you spot matches faster:

Characters Suit (🀟-🀧)

Numbers 1 through 9, marked with Chinese numerals (一, 二, 三...) and the character 萬 (ten-thousand). These are the most common tiles.

Bamboo Suit (🀹-🁁)

Numbers 1 through 9, depicted as bamboo stalks. Tile 1 is usually a bird (sparrow).

Dots Suit (🀰-🀸)

Numbers 1 through 9, shown as circular dots (coins).

Winds (🀘-🀛)

East (東), South (南), West (西), North (北). Only 4 of each exist per game.

Dragons (🀤-🀦)

Red Dragon (中), Green Dragon (發), White Dragon (白). Rare but important tiles.

Seasons & Flowers (🀨-🀯)

These are special bonus tiles. Unlike other tiles, any Season can match any Season and any Flower can match any Flower — they don't need to be identical.

🎯 Level Breakdown (All 10 Levels)

  • Level 1 — "First Steps": 36 tiles, simple grid layout. Introduction to basic matching.
  • Level 2 — "Learning the Ropes": 48 tiles, slight stacking introduced.
  • Level 3 — "Turtle Formation": 56 tiles, classic turtle layout. Multi-layer strategy required.
  • Level 4 — "Dragon's Tail": 72 tiles, long narrow layout. Edge-focused strategy.
  • Level 5 — "Fortress": 88 tiles, tall central stack. Requires planning 3-4 moves ahead.
  • Level 6 — "Phoenix Wings": 96 tiles, wide symmetrical layout. Time management becomes critical.
  • Level 7 — "Temple Gate": 108 tiles, deep layered stacks. Master-level puzzle.
  • Level 8 — "Emperor's Throne": 120 tiles, complex overlapping layers. Expert difficulty.
  • Level 9 — "Heaven's Pillar": 132 tiles, towering central stack. Only for seasoned players.
  • Level 10 — "Dragon's Crown": 144 tiles (full set), the ultimate challenge. Beat this to claim Grandmaster status!

🌟 Pro Tips & Strategies

🔎 Tip 1: Prioritize Unblocking Tiles

Always match tiles that free up the most other tiles. Removing a tile on a stack edge often unlocks 2-3 tiles underneath. Think two moves ahead — a good match now might be a trap if it blocks your next move.

📈 Tip 2: Scan for Quadruplets Early

If you spot 4 identical tiles, plan which two pairs to match first. Matching the "wrong" pair can trap the remaining two behind other tiles. Always match the most blocked pair first.

🎯 Tip 3: Focus on the Tallest Stack

The central/highest stack is usually the bottleneck. Clear it from the top down. Don't get distracted by easy matches on the edges — the center holds the key to completion.

⏲ Tip 4: Save Shuffles for Emergencies

Don't shuffle at the first sign of trouble. Exhaust your visible options first. A well-timed shuffle in Level 7-10 can save a run that has only 4-6 tiles left.

🚀 Tip 5: Speed Strategy

For leaderboard times, memorize common tile patterns. The Winds and Dragons tiles appear only in specific positions on each layout. Knowing where to find 發 on Level 3 saves precious seconds.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can every layout be solved?

Yes! All 10 levels in ZenMatch Mahjong Solitaire are guaranteed solvable. If you get stuck, use Shuffle or Hint to find a way forward.

Q: What happens if I run out of time?

There is no fail timer — you can take as long as you need. The timer tracks your speed for the leaderboard, but a slow completion still counts as a win.

Q: Do Seasons and Flowers match each other?

Yes — any Season matches any other Season, and any Flower matches any other Flower. They don't need to be the same number.

Q: How do I get on the leaderboard?

Complete levels with fast times! After beating Level 10, you can submit your total time to the global leaderboard. The faster you clear all levels, the higher you rank.
