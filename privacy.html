Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to DragonTile Games ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, transparent experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit dragontile.games and use our free online puzzle games.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information, and only when you choose to provide it:

  • Leaderboard Data: When you submit a score, you may provide a display name and your game score. This is stored solely to power our leaderboard feature. You can use any pseudonym you like.
  • Newsletter Subscription: If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address for the sole purpose of sending you updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Automatically Collected Data: Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may collect standard server logs including IP addresses and browser type for security and performance purposes. These logs are not used to track individual users.

3. What We DO NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do not do:

  • We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.
  • We do not display advertisements or use ad networks.
  • We do not use analytics trackers (Google Analytics, etc.).
  • We do not collect or sell personal data to third parties.
  • We do not require account registration.

4. How We Use Information

Any information you provide is used exclusively for the purpose it was given:

  • Leaderboard names and scores are displayed publicly on our leaderboard page.
  • Email addresses are used only to send newsletters. We do not share or sell your email.
  • Server logs are used for security monitoring and site availability only.

5. Data Storage & Security

Leaderboard data is stored on Cloudflare's D1 database, which provides enterprise-grade security. We use IP-based rate limiting to prevent abuse of our score submission API. While we implement reasonable security measures, no online service can guarantee 100% security.

6. Children's Privacy

DragonTile Games is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our site, please contact us and we will delete it promptly. Our games are suitable for all ages and require no personal information to play.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under laws such as GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA (California), and others, including:

  • The right to access any personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of communications

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

8. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process visitor IP addresses and browser metadata for security and Content Delivery Network (CDN) purposes. For more information, please review Cloudflare's privacy policy.

We use Google Fonts to serve typefaces. Google may collect usage data when fonts are loaded. You can review Google's privacy policy for details.

9. Cookie Policy

DragonTile Games does not set any cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics purposes. The only local storage we may use is your browser's localStorage to save game progress (such as completed levels and best scores) locally on your device. This data never leaves your browser.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@dragontile.games
  • Visit our Contact page
